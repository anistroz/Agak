FAQ

Questions clippers ask.

Do I need a big following?

No. Shorts, Reels and X push good clips to people who don't follow you. A strong hook in the first two seconds and posting fast matter far more than followers.

How much can I earn?

Each campaign shows its rate per 1,000 views. At ₹20 per 1,000, a clip with 50,000 views earns ₹1,000. Some campaigns have a minimum view count and a most-per-clip limit, both shown on the campaign.

When do I get paid?

Views are counted after the campaign's hold period, usually 7 days after you post. Once your clip is approved, the amount shows as owed in your dashboard, and payouts go to your UPI ID. You get a notification when the money is sent.

How are Instagram and X views counted?

YouTube views are read automatically. For Instagram and X, open the clip's Insights after the hold period, take a screenshot that shows the views, and add it to the clip in your dashboard.

Why was my clip refused?

The most common reasons: the clip is on a different account from the one in your profile, it was posted before the campaign started, it was already sent in, or it breaks a campaign rule (no credit, copyrighted music, a plain reupload). The reason is always shown on the clip.

Can I use more than one account?

One account per platform per profile. Add your YouTube, Instagram, X and TikTok handles, and you can post on all four.

What happens when the budget runs out?

The campaign closes on its own and stops taking new clips. Clips that were already approved are still paid.
