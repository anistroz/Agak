Last updated 18 November 2026

This policy explains what {{NAME}} ("ClipKontrast", "we") collects when you use {{SITE}}, why, who else handles it, how long we keep it and the choices you have. We act as the controller of this information.

What we collect

  • Account. Email address, name, and either a password (stored only as a one-way scrypt hash) or, with Sign in with Google, your Google account ID, name and email. Whether you have confirmed your email.
  • What you give us to clip. Links you paste, files you upload, creators and channel links you add, and your settings.
  • What we make from it. Downloaded videos, transcripts, loudness and chat-activity figures, clips, titles, captions and scores.
  • Public channel details. For channel links you add: the channel's public name, picture, follower count and live status from YouTube, Twitch or Kick.
  • Connected accounts. Access tokens from YouTube or Instagram if you connect them to post clips.
  • AI usage. A record of each AI check made for your account: when, what for and how many tokens it used.
  • Earn. If you join a paid campaign: your display name, WhatsApp number, UPI ID, city and the account handles you add; the clip links you send, their view counts and the proof screenshots you upload; and the payouts made to you (amount, UPI ID, payment reference and date).
  • Technical data. Session records with IP address and browser, and server logs, used for security and to keep the Service running.

Why we use it

  • To provide the Service you asked for: downloading, transcribing, judging, cutting, captioning and posting clips (performance of our contract with you).
  • To keep accounts secure, prevent fraud and abuse, and enforce our Terms (our legitimate interests).
  • To send account emails such as email confirmation and password resets (contract and legitimate interests).
  • For Earn: to check that a clip is on your own account, count its views, pay you by UPI and contact you about a payout on WhatsApp or by email (performance of our contract with you), and to keep payment records the law requires.

We do not sell your information, do not use it for advertising, and do not use your videos or clips to train AI models.

Who else handles it

  • Our AI provider receives transcript text and loudness and chat figures for the moments being judged, to choose clips and write titles and captions. Speech is transcribed on our own servers.
  • Our email provider delivers account emails.
  • Our hosting providers store and run the Service.
  • YouTube, Instagram, Twitch and Kick receive the requests needed to fetch content and public channel details and, when you choose, the clips you post.
  • Earn. YouTube receives the IDs of clips you send in, to read their views and channel. Our bank or UPI app receives your UPI ID and the amount when we pay you. Campaign leaderboards show everyone your display name (or, without one, the first two letters of your email), with your clip count, views and earnings. Your WhatsApp number, UPI ID and email are never shown to other users.
